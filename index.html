How adoption works

Four steps, all online, from application to bringing your furbaby home.

  1. Apply onlineFill out the adoption application for the furbaby you'd like to meet.
  2. We review itOur adoption team reads every application and gets in touch with you.
  3. Sign the contractOnce you're approved, you sign the adoption contract.
  4. Get the medical recordsWe email your furbaby's medical records to you.
